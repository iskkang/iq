About LandedIQ

Built by logistics operators, not just software developers.

LandedIQ was created to connect the parts of importing that are usually separated: HTS classification, tariff programs, freight allocation, landed cost, margin, and selling-price decisions.

Try Free HTS LookupRead the methodology

LandedIQ is developed and operated by MTL Co., Ltd., an international freight-forwarding and logistics company. The product grew from practical import workflows where a duty percentage alone was not enough: sellers needed to understand what the rate did to the true unit cost and the margin left after freight and channel fees.

Practical logistics experience

The workflow is designed around actual product lists, shipment costs, origins, freight allocation, duty layers, and SKU-level margin decisions.

Transparent calculations

Base MFN duty, additional tariff programs, fees, freight, and margin logic are shown separately so users can audit how the result was produced.

Effective-date awareness

Tariff treatment is evaluated using the HTS code, country of origin, and selected effective date instead of assuming that today’s rate always applied.

Why we built it

Import data is abundant. Decision-ready answers are not.

Official schedules and tariff actions contain the underlying rules, but importers still have to connect those rules to product costs, logistics expenses, channel fees, and target margin. LandedIQ turns those separate inputs into one auditable workflow.

It is not a replacement for a licensed customs broker or legal adviser. It is a working tool for identifying duty layers, testing landed-cost scenarios, and knowing which questions require professional confirmation.
