FAQ

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Two shipments and 25 SKUs, with no card required. That is enough to put your own products through classification, the duty stack, landed cost and pricing before deciding.

What does $29/mo include?

Unlimited shipments and SKUs, with every feature of the free plan. It is billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time from Stripe; access continues until the end of the paid period.

Where does the tariff data come from?

Base HTS and MFN records are sourced from official USITC exports. Section 301 coverage is evaluated from official HTS Chapter 99, including U.S. note 20. Coverage details and limitations are listed in the methodology.

Does LandedIQ replace a customs broker?

No. Suggested classifications and estimates must be reviewed. Final classification, admissibility and duty liability remain with the importer of record and its qualified advisers.
