Questions before you trust the number

Is this customs or legal advice?+

No. LandedIQ produces estimates for cost planning and pricing decisions. Final HTS classification and duty liability remain with the importer of record.

Where do the duty rates come from?+

Base MFN rates come from the official USITC Harmonized Tariff Schedule. Additional programs are maintained with effective dates and shown separately on every report.

What if the classification is uncertain?+

We flag uncertainty and show ranked candidates. Unresolved lines are not converted into a false zero or a confident wrong answer.

What do I need to upload?+

SKU, product name, material or description, unit cost, country of origin and units per shipment. Weight is optional.
