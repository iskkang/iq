What we collect

Your email address, submitted through the early-access form. Along with it we store the landing page variant you saw and any campaign parameters (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign) present in the URL you arrived from. That's all — no account, no payment data, no tracking cookies.

Solely to notify you about LandedIQ early access — your place in line, onboarding invitations, and the sample report if you requested one. We do not sell or share your email with third parties.

How it's processed

Form submissions are stored in our own database, hosted by Supabase in Canada (ca-central-1). They are not passed to any form-handling third party. Site analytics run on Plausible, which is cookieless and does not collect personal data.

Deleting your data

Want your email removed? Send a deletion request to support@landediq.app and we'll delete it within 7 days. Every email we send also includes an opt-out.
